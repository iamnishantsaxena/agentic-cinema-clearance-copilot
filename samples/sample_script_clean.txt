TITLE: THE LAST LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER

FADE IN:

EXT. CRAGMOOR LIGHTHOUSE - DUSK

A fictional rock spire on an unnamed coast. WIND howls. ELSA
VANTERPOOL (60s), weathered and sharp-eyed, climbs the spiral
stairs with a lantern.

                    ELSA
          Forty years I've kept this light.
          Not for the ships. For the quiet.

Her apprentice, TOBIAS REED (19), fumbles with a coil of rope.

                    TOBIAS
          My mother says the old stories
          about this place are just stories.

                    ELSA
          Your mother's never spent a winter
          up here alone.

A gull cries. Below, invented town BRACKENFELL glows with
lantern light, its harbor empty of any real ship or brand.

                    TOBIAS
          What happens if the light goes out?

                    ELSA
          Then we find out how dark the world
          really is.

She lights the lamp. The beam sweeps out over open water.

FADE OUT.
