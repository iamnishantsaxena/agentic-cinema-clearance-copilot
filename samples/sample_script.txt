TITLE: MIDNIGHT IN QUEENS

FADE IN:

BASED ON TRUE EVENTS.

INT. DINER - TIMES SQUARE - NIGHT

MARA (28), exhausted, slides into a booth across from JOEY (30s),
a wiry line cook still in his apron.

                    JOEY
          You know what my old man used to
          say? "The night is darkest just
          before the dawn." Guy stole that
          from somewhere, I bet.

                    MARA
          Everyone steals from someone.

A busboy sets down two cans of Coca-Cola. Mara cracks hers open.

                    MARA (CONT'D)
          I heard Elon Musk eats here when
          he's in town. Probably just a
          rumor.

                    JOEY
          Everything's a rumor till it's
          not.

Joey hums a few bars of "Bridge Over Troubled Water," off-key.

                    MARA
          Don't quit your day job.

He laughs. Outside the window, the neon glow of Times Square
flickers against the rain-streaked glass.

FADE OUT.
